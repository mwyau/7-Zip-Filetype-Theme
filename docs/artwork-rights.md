Every package includes the official 7-Zip license as `7-Zip-License.txt`. Redistribution of third-party artwork requires separate permission.

## Confirming redistribution rights

1. Check each artist's published licensing terms.
2. Where rights are unclear, seek permission to **redistribute the artwork inside modified 7-Zip DLL/EXE files**, including on GitHub Releases, and keep a dated record.
3. Confirm Ribbon source authorship and terms independently.
4. Update the table with public evidence links or permission records. Obtain consent before publishing private correspondence.
5. Preserve credits and upstream license notices in source and published artifacts.

Redistribution rights are still unverified. Do not label the artwork as public domain, open source, or freely redistributable without supporting terms or permission.
